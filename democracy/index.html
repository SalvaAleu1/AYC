Peaceful democratic participation

Democracy & Elections

AYC's constitutional rules for internal nominations, fair competition, campaign conduct, electoral disputes and responsible post-election behaviour.

Democratic principles

Leadership through legitimate choice.

The Constitution requires democracy, transparency, accountability, fairness and equal opportunity. Leadership may be attained only through democratic means.

Open nominations

Eligible aspirants must be treated equally. A Nomination Committee receives applications, verifies eligibility, conducts primaries where necessary and declares Party nominees.

One member, one vote

Where more than one eligible member seeks the same nomination, Party primaries are conducted democratically, normally by secret ballot, with each registered member entitled to one vote.

Issue-based campaigning

Campaigns are to be guided by honesty, respect, peaceful competition, issue-based debate, unity and integrity.

Campaign standards

Conduct AYC rejects.

Competitive politics must never become an excuse for intimidation, division or corruption.

Prohibited conductConstitutional standard
Vote buying or gifts intended to influence votingNomination and elections must be free from bribery and improper inducement.
Threats, violence or intimidationMembers and candidates must compete peacefully and respect fellow contestants.
Sectional, clan or discriminatory politicsCampaigning must preserve unity and reject discrimination.
False information or forged documentsHonesty and integrity govern campaign communication and Party records.
Misuse of Party resourcesParty property and funds must be used lawfully and accountably.

Dispute resolution

Fair process before final decisions.

Nomination disputes are first submitted to the Disciplinary Committee acting as the Party's Electoral Dispute Resolution Committee. Parties have the right to be heard, and dissatisfied members may appeal to the General Conference.

Following lawful nominations and elections, members are expected to respect the outcome and support the duly nominated candidate. Party-sponsored leaders who win AYU office are required to govern under the Constitution of the Apuk Youth Union and remain accountable to all Union members.

Political participation exists to strengthen the Apuk Youth Union and serve the wider Apuk community.
AYC post-election principle

Between election cycles

An electoral movement with a defined pause mechanism.

After an AYU election and assumption of office, AYC may suspend routine political and organizational activity for the elected term while preserving its identity, records, assets and membership. It resumes when the next electoral timetable is announced, or earlier through the constitutional process.

Apuk Youth for Change

AYC is an independent democratic youth alliance established by members of the Apuk Youth Union in Juba. It does not replace the Union.
