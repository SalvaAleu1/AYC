Peaceful democratic participation

Democracy & Elections

AYC supports fair competition, respectful participation and leadership chosen through peaceful democratic means.

Democratic principles

Leadership through legitimate choice.

AYC believes democratic participation should be transparent, respectful, fair and focused on ideas that serve the community.

Fair participation

Members and candidates should be treated with dignity and given a fair opportunity to participate.

Peaceful competition

Political competition should never become a reason for threats, intimidation, violence or division.

Issue-based campaigning

Campaigns should focus on leadership, priorities, service and the future of the community.

Campaign standards

Politics with responsibility.

AYC rejects practices that undermine trust, unity or the integrity of democratic participation.

No intimidation

Participation must remain peaceful and free from threats, harassment or coercion.

No divisive politics

AYC rejects discrimination and campaigning designed to divide the community along sectional or social lines.

No corruption

Votes, support and public trust should never be bought, manipulated or exchanged for improper benefit.

After the vote

Respect the outcome. Serve everyone.

Democratic leadership carries a responsibility to respect legitimate outcomes, maintain peace and serve beyond campaign lines. AYC supports leadership that remains accountable to the whole community.

Political participation should strengthen institutions, preserve unity and serve the wider Apuk community.
