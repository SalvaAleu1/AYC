Voluntary membership

Membership

Who may join AYC, the constitutional membership categories, and the rights and responsibilities attached to membership.

Eligibility

Membership begins with constitutional commitment.

Ordinary membership is open to registered members of the Apuk Youth Union in Juba who voluntarily accept the AYC Constitution and subscribe to its vision, mission, objectives, values and principles.

  • Be a registered member of the Apuk Youth Union in Juba.
  • Accept the Constitution of Apuk Youth for Change.
  • Believe in democracy and peaceful leadership.
  • Meet the age requirements prescribed by the Constitution of the Union.
  • Demonstrate good character and integrity.
Membership shall be voluntary, equal and free from discrimination within the constitutional framework of the Union.
AYC membership principle

Membership categories

Five constitutional categories.

Different categories recognize different relationships with the movement while preserving the rights and restrictions established by the Constitution.

Founding Members

Persons who participated in the establishment and adoption of the Constitution.

Ordinary Members

Registered AYU members who voluntarily join AYC.

Honorary Members

Distinguished persons recognized for exceptional service to the Apuk community or Party; voting depends on ordinary-member qualification.

Associate Members

Supporters of AYC objectives who do not qualify for ordinary membership; associate members may not contest Party office.

Life Members

Membership that may be conferred upon members who have rendered extraordinary service to the Party.

Rights & duties

Participation carries responsibility.

Membership rights protect democratic participation; duties protect unity, discipline and constitutional integrity.

Member rights

  • Participate in lawful Party activities and meetings.
  • Vote and contest Party positions subject to the Constitution.
  • Receive equal treatment and access Party information.
  • Propose policies, petition Party organs and criticize leadership constructively.
  • Appeal disciplinary decisions and resign voluntarily.

Member duties

  • Respect the AYC Constitution and uphold the Constitution of AYU.
  • Promote unity, discipline and democracy.
  • Reject corruption, tribalism and discrimination.
  • Participate in Party programmes and protect Party property.
  • Support duly nominated Party candidates.

How admission works

The Constitution requires membership applications in the prescribed form, consideration by the Membership Committee, entry of approved members into the official Membership Register and issuance of an official membership card.
