Eligibility

Membership is voluntary and values-based.

Ordinary membership is intended for eligible members of the Apuk Youth Union who identify with AYC's vision, mission, values and commitment to peaceful democratic participation.

  • Be eligible for membership of the Apuk Youth Union.
  • Support AYC's vision, mission and public values.
  • Believe in peaceful democratic leadership and responsible participation.
  • Demonstrate good character, respect and integrity.
  • Be willing to contribute positively to the movement and community.

AYC membership principle

Why join

Take part. Contribute. Serve.

Membership provides a way for eligible young people to engage in AYC's democratic, civic and community-focused work.

Participate

Engage in lawful AYC activities, discussions, programmes and democratic processes.

Contribute ideas

Help shape priorities, programmes and constructive approaches to leadership and community service.

Support the community

Take part in initiatives that promote unity, opportunity, welfare, peace and development.
