Apuk Youth for Change

Leadership

Constitutional leadership of Apuk Youth for Change and the offices responsible for the Party’s direction and administration.

AGA

Founding Chairperson

Agany Geng Ayiei

Chairperson · Apuk Youth for Change

The Constitution is assented to and promulgated by Agany Geng Ayiei in his capacity as Founding Chairperson. Under the Constitution, the Party Chairperson is the political leader and chief executive officer of AYC, provides strategic leadership, promotes unity and discipline, and serves as the official Party flag bearer for the office of Chairperson of the Apuk Youth Union in Juba.

The Chairperson's authority is expressly limited by constitutional safeguards: no unilateral amendment of the Constitution, dissolution of the Party, unauthorized expenditure or suspension of members outside due process.

Executive Committee

Ten constitutional offices.

The Executive Committee is the principal executive organ responsible for implementing decisions of the General Conference and managing the Party's day-to-day affairs.

Party ChairpersonPolitical leader, chief executive and official flag bearer.
Deputy Party ChairpersonAssists the Chairperson and serves as official running mate unless otherwise determined constitutionally.
Secretary GeneralAdministrative head, records custodian and coordinator of Party activities.
Deputy Secretary GeneralSupports the Secretary General and coordinates assigned programmes.
Secretary for Finance and PlanningBudgets, financial records, reporting and prudent resource management.
Secretary for Information and Public RelationsOfficial spokesperson, public communication, media engagement and publications.
Secretary for Legal AffairsLegal advice, constitutional interpretation, agreements and disciplinary coordination.
Secretary for Organization and MobilizationMembership registration, mobilization, events and campaign coordination.
Secretary for Education, Research and PolicyPolicy development, civic education, research and manifesto monitoring.
Secretary for Social Welfare and Community AffairsWelfare, humanitarian initiatives, health awareness and community outreach.

Accountability

Leadership is not unlimited authority.

Every office bearer remains accountable to the General Conference and is subject to constitutional standards of integrity, transparency, consultation and servant leadership.

Collective responsibility

Executive Committee members are required to uphold lawful decisions while using internal procedures to express disagreement.

Fair removal process

Removal for gross misconduct, abuse of office, corruption or serious constitutional violations requires notice, a fair opportunity to respond and a two-thirds decision of a duly convened General Conference.

Conflict-of-interest standards

Leaders must avoid conflicts of interest, declare relevant personal interests, safeguard confidential information and refrain from using office for personal benefit.

Apuk Youth for Change

Apuk Youth for Change (AYC) is an independent democratic youth alliance established by members of the Apuk Youth Union in Juba. It does not replace the Apuk Youth Union or exercise powers reserved to the Union.

Headquarters: Juba, Central Equatoria State, South Sudan.
