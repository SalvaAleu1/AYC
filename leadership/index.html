Leadership

Leadership grounded in service, responsibility, unity and accountability to the people we seek to serve.

AGA

Founding Chairperson

Agany Geng Ayiei

Chairperson · Apuk Youth for Change

Agany Geng Ayiei serves as the Founding Chairperson of Apuk Youth for Change. The Chairperson provides political and strategic leadership, promotes unity within the movement and represents AYC in its public and institutional engagements.

AYC's leadership philosophy emphasizes consultation, integrity, discipline, accountability and service to the wider Apuk community.

Leadership approach

Responsibility before title.

Leadership in AYC is expected to strengthen the movement, respect institutions and keep community interests at the centre of decision-making.

Strategic direction

Leaders are expected to keep AYC focused on its purpose, priorities and long-term contribution to the community.

Collective leadership

AYC values consultation, teamwork and responsible coordination across its leadership and membership.

Public accountability

Office bearers are expected to act honestly, avoid conflicts of interest and use their positions for service rather than personal advantage.

Leadership values

Standards that matter.

AYC's public leadership is guided by principles that support trust, fairness and effective service.

Integrity

Act truthfully, protect public trust and uphold high ethical standards.

Accountability

Accept responsibility for decisions, conduct and the stewardship of movement resources.

Unity

Lead in a way that brings people together and rejects divisive politics.

Service

Keep the interests of members and the wider community ahead of personal interests.

Apuk Youth for Change

Apuk Youth for Change (AYC) is an independent democratic youth alliance established by members of the Apuk Youth Union. It does not replace the Union or exercise powers reserved to it.

Headquarters: Juba, Central Equatoria State, South Sudan.
