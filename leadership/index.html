Agany Geng Ayiei is recognized as the founding Chairperson of Apuk Youth for Change. His role belongs to the party's founding history, while the current administration is led by the interim leadership listed above.

AYC traces its origin to the campaign platform established ahead of the 2024 Apuk Youth Union elections.

Apuk Youth for Change

Apuk Youth for Change (AYC) is a youth political party and forum that forms part of the Apuk Youth Union (AYU) in Juba, South Sudan. It was founded as a campaign platform ahead of the 2024 elections and operates under the motto “Committed to Good.”

Location: Juba, Central Equatoria State, South Sudan.
