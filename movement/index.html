Apuk Youth for Change

Our Movement

The constitutional identity, purpose, values and symbolism of Apuk Youth for Change.

Who we are

A democratic alliance — not the Apuk Youth Union.

Apuk Youth for Change is a voluntary democratic youth alliance established by registered members of the Apuk Youth Union in Juba. Its role is electoral and organizational: to participate in Union leadership elections, promote good governance and sponsor competent leaders.

AYC operates solely within the framework of the Constitution of the Apuk Youth Union in Juba. It does not replace the Union and does not assume powers that belong to the Union.

“Committed to Good.”
Official motto of Apuk Youth for Change

Direction

Vision and mission.

The Constitution places community welfare, youth opportunity and responsible leadership at the centre of AYC's purpose.

Vision

To build a united, peaceful, educated, prosperous, and self-reliant Apuk community where every young person has an equal opportunity to realize their potential through accountable leadership, social justice, innovation, and community service.

Mission

To provide visionary, servant, and accountable leadership that promotes unity, education, youth empowerment, economic transformation, social welfare, cultural preservation, and sustainable community development while faithfully implementing the Constitution and objectives of the Apuk Youth Union in Juba.

Service principle

Party leaders are required to place community interests above personal interests, serve honestly, protect public resources, respect members equally, promote reconciliation and exercise authority with humility.

Core values

The standards AYC sets for itself.

These principles guide Party decisions, conduct and public service.

Integrity & accountability

Integrity, accountability, transparency, ethical leadership and protection of public resources.

Unity & dignity

Unity, equality, inclusiveness, respect for human dignity and peaceful coexistence.

Democracy & law

Democratic governance, constitutionalism, rule of law, public participation and peaceful political competition.

Service & patriotism

Patriotism, volunteerism, community service and service above self.

Youth opportunity

Youth empowerment, education, innovation, economic advancement and equal opportunity.

Commitment to good

The official motto is also a constitutional value: a commitment to ethical leadership, responsible action and community progress.

The emblem

Identity with meaning.

The Party's official emblem and colours are constitutionally defined and protected as part of AYC's identity.

Hawk

Courage, vigilance, resilience, vision, strength and responsible leadership.

Upward growth chart

Development, economic progress, innovation, transformation and continuous advancement.

Handshake

Peace, unity, reconciliation, mutual respect, cooperation, dialogue and collective responsibility.

Laurel branches

Honour, achievement, peace and victory through democratic means.

Circular design

Unity, equality, inclusiveness, continuity and enduring solidarity.
