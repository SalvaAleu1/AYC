Apuk Youth for Change

Our Movement

The history, identity and public purpose of Apuk Youth for Change.

Who we are

A youth political party and forum within AYU.

Apuk Youth for Change (AYC) is a youth political party and forum that forms part of the Apuk Youth Union (AYU) in Juba, South Sudan.

AYC brings members together around political participation, leadership, public issues and community-focused priorities within the wider Apuk Youth Union environment.

“Committed to Good.”
Foundational motto of Apuk Youth for Change

Our beginning

Founded ahead of the 2024 elections.

AYC was originally founded as a campaign platform ahead of the 2024 Apuk Youth Union elections. The platform later developed into a youth political party and forum that continues to organize within the wider AYU community.

Its historical foundation remains closely associated with the motto “Committed to Good,” which continues to define the party's public identity.

Public direction

Vision and mission.

AYC's public direction focuses on youth opportunity, unity, responsible leadership and community progress.

Vision

To contribute to a united, peaceful, educated, prosperous and self-reliant Apuk community in which young people can realize their potential.

Mission

To provide a political and civic platform for responsible youth leadership, participation, opportunity, service and community-focused development.

Public service

AYC emphasizes leadership that respects people, encourages dialogue and keeps community interests at the centre of public engagement.

Core values

Standards that guide AYC.

These values describe the principles AYC presents publicly in its leadership and community engagement.

Integrity & accountability

Honest leadership, transparency and responsibility.

Unity & dignity

Respect, inclusion and peaceful coexistence.

Democratic participation

Peaceful political participation and respect for legitimate institutions.

Service

Community-minded leadership and responsible public engagement.

Youth opportunity

Education, skills, innovation and economic participation.

Committed to Good

The foundational motto that has accompanied AYC since its establishment.
