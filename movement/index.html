Apuk Youth for Change

Our Movement

Our identity, purpose, vision and values as a democratic youth alliance committed to responsible leadership and community progress.

Who we are

A democratic alliance — distinct from the Apuk Youth Union.

Apuk Youth for Change is a voluntary democratic youth alliance formed by members of the Apuk Youth Union. AYC brings people together around responsible leadership, peaceful democratic participation, youth opportunity and community service.

AYC does not replace the Apuk Youth Union. The two have different roles: the Union is the umbrella youth organization, while AYC is an alliance that organizes members around leadership, ideas and electoral participation.

“Committed to Good.”
Official motto of Apuk Youth for Change

Direction

Vision and mission.

AYC places community welfare, youth opportunity, unity and responsible leadership at the centre of its work.

Vision

To build a united, peaceful, educated, prosperous and self-reliant Apuk community where every young person has an equal opportunity to realize their potential through accountable leadership, social justice, innovation and community service.

Mission

To provide visionary, servant and accountable leadership that promotes unity, education, youth empowerment, economic transformation, social welfare, cultural preservation and sustainable community development.

Service principle

AYC promotes leadership that places community interests first, respects people equally, encourages reconciliation and exercises responsibility with humility.

Core values

The standards that guide AYC.

These values shape our public conduct, leadership and community engagement.

Integrity & accountability

Honest leadership, transparency, responsibility and care for public resources.

Unity & dignity

Equality, inclusion, respect for human dignity and peaceful coexistence.

Democracy & participation

Peaceful political competition, public participation and respect for legitimate institutions.

Service & patriotism

Community service, volunteerism, responsibility and service above self.

Youth opportunity

Education, innovation, skills, economic advancement and equal opportunity.

Commitment to good

A shared commitment to ethical leadership, responsible action and meaningful community progress.

Apuk Youth for Change

Apuk Youth for Change (AYC) is an independent democratic youth alliance established by members of the Apuk Youth Union. It does not replace the Union or exercise powers reserved to it.
