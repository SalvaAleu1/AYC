Privacy Policy

How the Apuk Youth for Change website approaches visitor privacy and personal information.

Apuk Youth for Change respects the privacy of visitors and members. This policy applies to information handled through this public website and linked official services.

1. Information we may receive
The public website can be viewed without creating an account. Where a visitor chooses to submit information through an official form or linked service, information is provided voluntarily for the purpose stated at the point of collection.
2. Technical information
Hosting and security providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request logs and security events to deliver and protect the website.
3. How information is used
Information received through official channels may be used to respond to requests, support membership or organizational services, maintain security, communicate with participants and meet legitimate administrative or legal obligations.
4. Sharing and disclosure
AYC does not publish private membership information through this website. Information may be handled by authorized service providers where necessary to deliver an official service, or disclosed where required by applicable law.
5. Security
Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss. No online service can guarantee absolute security.
6. Retention
Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was obtained, subject to legitimate administrative or legal record-keeping needs.
7. Policy changes
AYC may update this policy when website functions, linked services or applicable requirements change. The revision date shown on this page identifies the current public version.
