Privacy Policy

How this public website approaches visitor privacy and personal information.

Apuk Youth for Change respects the privacy of visitors and members. This policy applies to information handled through this public website.

1. Information this website may receive
The public pages can be viewed without creating an account. Where an authorized website function asks a visitor to submit personal information, the purpose of collection should be stated at the point of submission. Membership records are governed separately by the Party Constitution and authorized Party procedures.
2. Technical information
Hosting and security providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request logs and security events to deliver and protect the website.
3. How information is used
Information received through official website functions may be used to respond to requests, administer authorized Party services, maintain security, comply with lawful obligations and support legitimate organizational operations.
4. Sharing and disclosure
AYC does not publish private membership information through this website. Information may be shared with authorized service providers where necessary to operate the website, or where disclosure is required by applicable law or a competent authority.
5. Security
Reasonable administrative and technical measures should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss. No online system can guarantee absolute security.
6. Retention
Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was obtained, subject to constitutional, administrative, financial or legal record-keeping requirements.
7. Policy changes
AYC may update this policy when website functions or applicable requirements change. The revision date shown on this page identifies the current public version.
