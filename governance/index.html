Constitutional governance

Governance & Accountability

The institutions, safeguards and ethical standards that govern how AYC exercises authority and manages resources.

Party organs

Authority is distributed, not concentrated.

The Constitution creates distinct organs with defined functions and accountability to members.

General Conference

The supreme decision-making organ consisting of registered members. It approves or amends the Constitution, approves the manifesto, elects office bearers and receives Party reports.

Executive Committee

The principal executive organ that implements General Conference decisions, coordinates activities, supervises committees, oversees finances and administers Party affairs.

Advisory Council

A seven-member advisory body of respected senior members that promotes unity, mediates disputes, mentors leaders and provides strategic guidance without running day-to-day administration.

Standing Committees

Campaign and Strategy; Finance and Resource Mobilization; Disciplinary; Constitutional and Legal Affairs; Membership and Mobilization; and Manifesto Implementation and Monitoring.

Disciplinary Committee

A five-member body responsible for fair complaint investigation, hearings, findings, recommended sanctions and reconciliation where appropriate.

Member oversight

Members have rights to Party information, policy proposals, petitions, constructive criticism and appeal against disciplinary decisions.

Financial safeguards

Party resources are held in trust.

Financial management is constitutionally tied to transparency, accountability, integrity, prudence and value for money.

SafeguardRequirement
Official bank accountParty monies are to be deposited in an account held in the name of Apuk Youth for Change, not personal accounts.
Three signatoriesChairperson, Secretary for Finance and Planning, and Secretary General; payments require any two authorized signatures.
Documented expenditureExpenditures must be authorized, recorded and supported by appropriate documentation.
Financial recordsIncome, expenditure, assets, liabilities, donations, contributions, statements and property inventories must be maintained.
AuditAn Internal Audit Committee independent of bank signatories reviews financial affairs; an independent external auditor may also be appointed.
Member scrutinyFinancial reports are presented to the General Conference and members may seek clarification through established procedures.

Ethics & discipline

Fairness is part of accountability.

Members and leaders are expected to uphold the Constitution, promote unity, demonstrate honesty, avoid conflicts of interest, protect confidential information and reject abuse of office. Disciplinary proceedings must respect natural justice: allegations must be communicated and the member must have a reasonable opportunity to respond.

The Constitution also protects good-faith whistle-blowers reporting corruption, abuse of office, financial mismanagement or other serious misconduct from retaliation.

Authority must be exercised with humility, impartiality and accountability.
