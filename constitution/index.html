Governing instrument

Constitution

The supreme governing instrument of Apuk Youth for Change and the source of the Party's institutional framework.

Official document

The rules that govern AYC.

The Constitution defines AYC's identity, objectives, membership, organs, leadership, electoral procedures, financial controls, discipline and post-election status.

It expressly recognizes the Constitution of the Apuk Youth Union in Juba as the prevailing framework wherever the two instruments conflict.

What it covers

A complete institutional framework.

The Constitution establishes both democratic authority and the safeguards that constrain it.

Identity & principles

Name, motto, vision, mission, core values, legal status, headquarters, language, colours, emblem, flag and seal.

Membership

Eligibility, categories, admission, register, rights, duties, oath, fees, suspension, resignation, expulsion and reinstatement.

Organs & leadership

General Conference, Executive Committee, Advisory Council, committees, office bearers, powers, duties and accountability.

Elections & nominations

Democratic principles, candidate qualifications, nominations, primaries, campaign ethics, dispute resolution and post-election conduct.

Finance & property

Funding, banking, budgeting, expenditure, records, audit, property protection and financial transparency.

Discipline & ethics

Code of conduct, offences, hearings, sanctions, appeals, dispute resolution, whistle-blower protection and reconciliation.

Constitutional hierarchy

AYC remains distinct from AYU.

AYC is an independent democratic youth alliance formed by members of the Apuk Youth Union in Juba for electoral participation and promotion of good governance. It does not replace the Union or assume powers reserved to it. Party-sponsored leaders elected into AYU office must uphold the AYU Constitution and serve all members of the Union.
