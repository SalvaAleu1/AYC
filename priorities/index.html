Constitutional objectives

Our Priorities

A public framework drawn from the objectives adopted in the AYC Constitution. It does not replace a manifesto formally approved by the General Conference.

01 · Education & skills

Expand opportunity through learning.

AYC seeks improved educational opportunities through scholarships, mentorship and academic support, alongside vocational training, skills development, innovation and digital literacy.

02 · Economic opportunity

Enterprise, investment and employment.

The Party promotes entrepreneurship, youth economic empowerment, investment initiatives that benefit members and advocacy for employment opportunities for young people.

03 · Peace & welfare

Unity, reconciliation and care.

Peaceful coexistence, reconciliation, health awareness, support for vulnerable youth and families, volunteerism and community service are central priorities.

04 · Good governance

Transparent and accountable institutions.

AYC supports transparency in Union affairs, lawful resolutions, responsible leadership, public participation and peaceful democratic elections.

05 · Culture, sport & inclusion

Preserve identity and grow talent.

The objectives include preservation of Apuk culture, language and heritage, sports and talent development, and stronger participation of women in leadership.

06 · Sustainable development

Healthy communities and partnerships.

Environmental protection, sanitation and constructive partnerships with government institutions, civil society, the private sector and development partners support long-term community progress.

From priorities to policy

Commitments should be measurable.

AYC's constitutional structure assigns policy development, civic education, research and manifesto monitoring to the Secretary for Education, Research and Policy. Any election manifesto approved through the Party's constitutional processes can therefore be published and tracked against these foundational objectives.

Progress must serve the community, strengthen institutions and remain accountable to members.
