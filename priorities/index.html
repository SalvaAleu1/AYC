Our public agenda

Our Priorities

Practical areas where youth leadership can strengthen opportunity, unity, accountability and community development.

01 · Education & skills

Expand opportunity through learning.

AYC supports scholarships, mentorship, academic support, vocational training, innovation and digital literacy that help young people build useful skills and stronger futures.

02 · Economic opportunity

Enterprise, investment and employment.

AYC promotes entrepreneurship, employability, youth economic empowerment and constructive initiatives that expand opportunity.

03 · Peace & welfare

Unity, reconciliation and care.

Peaceful coexistence, reconciliation, health awareness, volunteerism and support for vulnerable members of the community remain important priorities.

04 · Good governance

Responsible and accountable leadership.

AYC supports transparency, public participation, ethical leadership and peaceful democratic engagement.

05 · Culture, sport & inclusion

Preserve identity and grow talent.

AYC values Apuk culture and language, sports and talent development, and wider participation in leadership and community life.

06 · Sustainable development

Healthy communities and strong partnerships.

Environmental responsibility, sanitation and constructive partnerships can support long-term community progress.

From priorities to action

Progress should be practical and measurable.

AYC aims to turn public priorities into clear programmes, partnerships and leadership commitments that can be understood, discussed and assessed by members and the wider community.

Progress must serve the community, strengthen institutions and remain accountable to people.
AYC public policy principle
Apuk Youth for Change

Apuk Youth for Change (AYC) is an independent democratic youth alliance established by members of the Apuk Youth Union. It does not replace the Union or exercise powers reserved to it.

Headquarters: Juba, Central Equatoria State, South Sudan.
