Youth Political Party & Forum

Team Committed to Good.

Apuk Youth for Change (AYC) forms part of the Apuk Youth Union (AYU) in Juba, South Sudan. Founded as a campaign platform ahead of the 2024 elections, AYC continues as a youth political party and forum under the foundational motto “Committed to Good.”

About AYC

A youth political party and forum within the Apuk Youth Union.

AYC provides a platform for members to organize around leadership, public issues, democratic participation and community-focused priorities within the wider Apuk Youth Union framework.

Origins

AYC was founded as a campaign platform ahead of the 2024 Apuk Youth Union elections.

Identity

AYC operates as a youth political party and forum forming part of the Apuk Youth Union in Juba.

Motto

The party's foundational motto is “Committed to Good.”

Public priorities

Areas of focus.

AYC's public priorities include education and skills, economic opportunity, peace and social welfare, accountable leadership, culture and sports, and sustainable community development.

Education, skills & digital opportunity

Education, mentorship, vocational skills, innovation and digital literacy.

Economic opportunity

Entrepreneurship, employability, investment and youth economic participation.

Peace & social welfare

Unity, reconciliation, health awareness, volunteerism and community support.

Governance & participation

Responsible leadership, public participation and peaceful democratic engagement.

AYC within AYU

Part of the wider Apuk Youth Union community.

Apuk Youth Union is the umbrella youth organization. Apuk Youth for Change is a youth political party and forum within that wider Union environment, with its own leadership, political identity and public priorities.

Apuk Youth Union

The umbrella youth organization serving the wider Apuk youth membership in Juba.

Apuk Youth for Change

A youth political party and forum that forms part of AYU and participates in its political and leadership processes.

2024 foundation

AYC began as a campaign platform ahead of the 2024 elections and developed into the party and forum it is today.
