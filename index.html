Our purpose

A movement for democratic choice and responsible leadership.

AYC exists to prepare, organize and sponsor competent leaders for elections of the Apuk Youth Union in Juba, while respecting the Union's Constitution and institutional authority.

Unity without division

AYC promotes reconciliation, inclusion and equal participation across sections, clans, religions, genders and social backgrounds.

Constitutional priorities

Six areas of action.

These areas organize the Party's constitutional objectives into a clear public framework. They do not replace any manifesto formally approved by the General Conference.

Priority 01

Education, skills & digital opportunity

Scholarships, mentorship, academic support, vocational training, innovation and digital literacy.

Priority 02

Jobs, enterprise & economic empowerment

Entrepreneurship, investment initiatives, employment opportunities and economic advancement for youth.

Priority 03

Peace, unity & social welfare

Reconciliation, peaceful coexistence, support for vulnerable people, health awareness and community service.

Priority 04

Good governance & accountability

Transparent Union affairs, ethical leadership, constitutionalism, peaceful elections and public participation.

Priority 05

Culture, sports & inclusive leadership

Preservation of Apuk culture and language, talent development, volunteerism and women's participation.

Priority 06

Environment & community development

Environmental protection, sanitation, partnerships and sustainable community development.

AYC and AYU

Different roles. One constitutional framework.

AYU is the umbrella youth organization. AYC is a democratic alliance established by AYU members to participate in leadership elections and promote good governance within the Union.

Apuk Youth Union in Juba

The umbrella organization governed by its own Constitution. Its institutional authority is not replaced by AYC.

Apuk Youth for Change

An independent democratic youth alliance that organizes, nominates and sponsors candidates for AYU leadership elections.

Constitutional respect

Where the AYC Constitution conflicts with the Constitution of AYU, the Constitution of the Union prevails.

Membership is voluntary.

Eligible members of the Apuk Youth Union in Juba may join AYC by subscribing to its vision, mission, objectives, values and Constitution.
