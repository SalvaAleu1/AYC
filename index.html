Vision & mission

A peaceful, educated, prosperous and self-reliant Apuk community.

  • Visionary, servant and accountable leadership.
  • Equal opportunity for young people to realize their potential.
  • Education, economic transformation and social welfare.
  • Cultural preservation and sustainable community development.

Public priorities

Six areas of action.

Our priorities focus on practical areas where youth leadership can help strengthen opportunity, unity, participation and community development.

Priority 01

Education, skills & digital opportunity

Scholarships, mentorship, academic support, vocational training, innovation and digital literacy.

Priority 02

Jobs, enterprise & economic empowerment

Entrepreneurship, investment, employability and economic opportunity for young people.

Priority 03

Peace, unity & social welfare

Reconciliation, peaceful coexistence, health awareness, volunteerism and community support.

Priority 04

Good governance & accountability

Responsible leadership, transparency, public participation and peaceful democratic engagement.

Priority 05

Culture, sports & inclusive leadership

Culture, language, talent development, sports and broader participation in leadership.

Priority 06

Environment & community development

Environmental responsibility, sanitation, partnerships and sustainable community progress.

AYC and AYU

Different roles. Shared respect for the community.

Apuk Youth Union is the umbrella youth organization. Apuk Youth for Change is a democratic alliance formed by Union members to participate in leadership elections, advance ideas and promote responsible leadership.

Apuk Youth Union

The umbrella youth organization serving the wider membership and community.

Apuk Youth for Change

A democratic youth alliance that organizes members, develops leadership and participates in Union elections.

Respect for institutions

AYC supports strong institutions, peaceful participation and responsible engagement with the Union and the wider community.

Apuk Youth for Change

Apuk Youth for Change (AYC) is an independent democratic youth alliance established by members of the Apuk Youth Union. It does not replace the Union or exercise powers reserved to it.
